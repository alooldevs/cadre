# warmup — before anything is a project

The stage before groundwork. Nothing is decided, nothing is owed, and there may not even be a folder.
Mode is `exploring`: output goes to the **sky**, and producing backlog items, anchor drafts, or plans
is the failure — convergence here is premature by definition.

## What this session is

- **Deliver the sky, not a roadmap.** What exists, what's possible, what nobody has done, options held
  open with tradeoffs. The human curates; a converged plan presented with authority steers them even
  when the research under it is thin.
- **No commitments.** No "we should", no scaffolding, no files created for structure's sake. Ideas stay
  ideas until the human promotes one.
- **Research is welcome.** Looking things up, comparing, mapping a territory — all in scope. Building is not.

## Where output lands

- If a repo/folder exists: its **sky** file (create on first use).
- If nothing exists yet: the conversation is the medium; when something is worth keeping, offer once to
  write it down and let the human say where.

## The exit

When one idea crystallizes into "this might be a thing," say so in one line and hand off:
**`groundwork`** — the outer rings get made and the idea becomes an anchor. Never slide there silently;
warmup ending is the human's call, not a drift.
