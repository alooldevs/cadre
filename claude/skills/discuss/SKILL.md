# discuss — step out of the work, anywhere

The mid-anything counterpart to `warmup`. Work is the default and needs no word; **stepping out of
work is the exception, so it gets one.** Saying `discuss` (or `brainstorm`) flips the session to
`exploring` mode wherever it stands — mid-build, mid-fix, day one or day ninety.

## What flips

- **Output goes to the sky.** Not the anchor, not the backlog, not the code.
- **The queue is suspended.** Nothing said here becomes a backlog item, a plan, or a build — however
  actionable it sounds. A good conversation silently becoming scope is the failure this word prevents.
- **Deliver the sky, not a roadmap.** Options held open with tradeoffs; what exists, what's possible.
  The human curates. Premature convergence is the failure, not slow progress.
- Research is in scope. Building is not.

## The exit — explicit, theirs

The session leaves discuss only when the human ends it, and the close is one question:
**"promote anything?"** They name what graduates — to the **anchor** (a decided direction), the
**slices** register (a shape/milestone change), or the **backlog** (a scoped item). Everything else
stays in the sky, un-promoted, retrievable. Never promote silently; never re-ask about what they
left behind.

If work was interrupted mid-task, resume it where it stood — the discussion changed nothing unless
something was promoted.

If what got promoted changes the shape of the work — a new slice, a scope big enough to be its own
track — say so in one line as the door closes. Naming what just became possible is part of the exit;
a capability nobody is told about does not exist.
